==================================================
Title: Feature Selection
Content:

- digit_count
- hyphen_count
- url_length

- has_http
- has_ip_host
- tld_is_suspicious

Selection method:
- Mutual Information (SelectKBest)
- Top-K features selected using sidebar setting

Speaker notes:
- This improves model focus by reducing less informative inputs.

==================================================
Q&A PREP (Expected Questions)
==================================================
Q1. Why not deep learning?
A: For CA-2, we prioritized explainability, speed, and easy deployment.

Q2. Why is precision/recall important?
A: In phishing detection, false negatives and false positives both matter.

Q3. How do you prevent overfitting?
A: We use train-test split, feature selection, and stratified K-fold validation.

Q4. Is this production-ready?
A: No, this is an educational prototype; production requires larger real datasets and stronger security checks.
